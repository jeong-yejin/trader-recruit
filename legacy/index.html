How the arena works.

E-sports format, perps engine. Four traders, one clock, one screen — and a room full of people watching every fill.

01 / Format

Four traders, one clock

All four trade the same session, same window, same venue-approved pairs. Everyone starts flat.

02 / Capital

Capital is provided

You don't bring your own bankroll. Each seat is funded with an identical starting balance for the session.

03 / Scoring

Live PnL leaderboard

Ranking is by session PnL, projected live on the main screen. Risk limits and max leverage are announced at briefing.

04 / Broadcast

Your screen is the show

Positions, entries and liquidation levels are on the big display. Commentary runs over the top, e-sports style.

05 / The crowd

The room has a vote

Attendees vote on who owns the arena before the bell. The crowd doesn't move your PnL — but it moves the room.

06 / The venue

Korea Blockchain Week

September 28, 2026 in Seoul, on the KBW 2026 week calendar, alongside the PERP-DEX DAY main stage and partner sessions.

Questions.

Anything not covered here — mail us and we'll answer directly.

Do I trade my own money?+

No. Each of the four seats is funded with an identical starting balance for the session. You are not asked to deposit, and you are not liable for session losses.

What exactly does the winner get?+

Qualification for the live trading competition in Singapore, plus round-trip airfare and accommodation for the competition dates, arranged and paid by the organiser. Visa support is provided where needed; the passport is on you. Only first place receives this — there is no second, third or fourth prize.

Is this open to traders outside Korea?+

Yes — but the arena is offline in Seoul on September 28, 2026, and you need to be in the room. Tell us where you're based in the form and we'll talk about travel.

What do I send as proof of track record?+

A shareable exchange PnL page, a public leaderboard profile, or a read-only API key. Never send a withdrawal-enabled key, a seed phrase, or a password — we will never ask for one, and any message that does is not from us.

Which pairs and what leverage?+

The approved pair list, maximum leverage and risk limits are fixed at the briefing the day before and are identical for all four seats. Breaching a limit is disqualifying.

Do I need to speak English?+

No. The interview and the event run in Korean or English, whichever you prefer. Interpretation is available on stage.

Can I bring my own setup?+

Your own keyboard and mouse, yes. Machines and screens are provided and identical, and the session runs on the venue network.
